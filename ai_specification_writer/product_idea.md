# Product Idea: CarpoolConnect

## Vision
Make the daily commute to work cheaper, greener, and less stressful by matching colleagues who travel similar routes into shared rides, managed and rewarded at the company level.

**Problem:** Most employees drive alone to the office. This costs them fuel and parking, creates congestion around company sites, and adds to the company's carbon footprint. Consumer ride-sharing apps don't solve this well because they match strangers, ignore office schedules, and give employers no visibility or control.

**Solution:** CarpoolConnect is a corporate commute management platform. Employees of the same company (or business park) are matched into recurring carpools based on home location, office location, and working hours. Employers get a dashboard to track participation, savings, and CO2 reduction, and can fund incentives.

## Users

### Primary users
- **Drivers (employees with a car)**
  - Want to share fuel and parking costs.
  - Want riders whose schedule and route fit theirs with minimal detour.
  - Want to ride with trusted, verified colleagues only.
- **Passengers (employees without a car or who prefer not to drive)**
  - Want an affordable, reliable ride to work at the times they need.
  - Want to know in advance who is picking them up and when.
  - Want a fallback if their driver cancels.

### Secondary users
- **HR / Facilities managers (company admins)**
  - Want to reduce parking demand and support employee well-being.
  - Want reports on participation, cost savings, and CO2 reduction for ESG reporting.
  - Want to set company policies (eligible sites, incentives, who can join).
- **Platform administrators (CarpoolConnect team)**
  - Want to onboard companies, manage accounts, and handle disputes and safety reports.

## Key Features
- **Corporate sign-up and verification:** employees join with their company email (SSO where available), so every rider is a verified colleague.
- **Smart route and schedule matching:** matches drivers and passengers by home area, office site, arrival/departure times, and acceptable detour.
- **Recurring carpools:** set up a weekly commute once (e.g. Mon–Thu, 08:30 arrival) instead of booking every day.
- **One-off ride requests:** request or offer a single ride for days outside the regular schedule.
- **Real-time trip updates:** pickup reminders, live driver location on the trip day, and delay/cancellation notifications.
- **Cost sharing:** automatic fair split of fuel cost per trip, settled in-app or through payroll deduction.
- **Ratings and safety:** mutual ratings after trips, report/block options, and emergency contact sharing.
- **Company admin dashboard:** participation stats, parking spaces freed, money saved, and CO2 avoided, exportable for ESG reports.
- **Incentives and gamification:** company-funded rewards (priority parking, points, monthly prizes) for regular carpoolers.
- **Guaranteed ride home:** if a driver cancels, the passenger can claim a company-paid taxi up to a set limit.

## Differentiators
- **Closed, trusted network:** only verified colleagues, unlike public ride-sharing apps that match strangers.
- **Built around work schedules:** recurring commutes and shift patterns instead of on-demand trips.
- **Employer value built in:** dashboards, policy controls, and ESG-ready CO2 reporting give companies a reason to pay for and promote it.
- **Payroll-integrated cost sharing:** no cash handling between colleagues.
- **Guaranteed ride home:** removes the main fear that stops people from giving up their car.

## Success Metrics
- % of employees at a site who carpool at least once a week.
- Average occupancy per participating car.
- Parking demand reduction at participating sites.
- Estimated CO2 avoided per month.
- Driver/passenger retention after 3 months.

## Out of Scope (for MVP)
- Public (non-corporate) ride-sharing.
- Professional drivers or paid taxi services (except the guaranteed ride home).
- Multi-modal journey planning (public transport, bikes).
